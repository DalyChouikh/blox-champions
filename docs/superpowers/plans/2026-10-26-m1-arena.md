# M1 Overhaul, Part 1: The Arena — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The generated arena takes Roller Champions' shape, with an open top and glass barriers that keep every skater in. It also gets glass arches at the gates, a beam beside the ball carrier, and the goal hanging over the track.

**Architecture:**
- **The pipeline stays.** The pure `Arena/Geometry` describes every piece from `Config.Arena`, and `server/Arena/Builder` turns the pieces into tagged parts. Client modules find what they light by tag.
- **The roof goes.** In its place come two vertical glass strips swept around the loop, tagged `Barrier`. Only the collider sweep sees them; every ray looks through them.
- **One bounds rule** (`Bounds.inArena`, the box inside the glass) judges both the ball and skaters.
- **One reach rule** (`Geometry/Reach`) proves at build time that no skater can clear the glass.

**Tech Stack:** Luau (`--!strict`), Rojo 7.7.1, StyLua 2.6.0, selene 0.32.0, luau-lsp 1.70.1, Jest Roblox 3.20.1, React Luau 17.3.13; CI with Python 3 (standard library) and Ruff 0.17.2.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-m1-arena-design.md` (approved 2026-10-26).
- It amends `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-21-m1-foundation-core-match-design.md` (decision A7).
- The reference is `docs/reference/2026-10-25-reference-games.md`.

## Verified facts this plan relies on (2026-10-26)

1. **Today's arena, from the pure geometry** (Studio probe on a clone of `Shared`):
   - 15,666 parts: surface 7,334, roof 8,106, infield 193, rim 16, gates 4, markers 4, the panel and 8 spawns;
   - 15,360 of them are wedges;
   - `Geometry.build` takes 16 ms.
2. **The new profile's strip counts** (the same 64-segment lengths and largest-remainder rule as `Profile.stripCounts`):
   - `stripsPerSection` 20 gives `{ 5, 1, 5, 1, 7, 1 }` for the bank, lower floor, step, upper floor, foot curve and outer wall;
   - that is 21 points, from (0, 8) to (60, 28).
3. **Heights:**
   - a skater never moves faster than `Movement.hardCap` (140), in the air too, actions included (`SkateMath`);
   - a ball-sized part launched straight up in Play rose 47.9 studs at 140 studs/s and 127.9 at 230;
   - `Workspace.Gravity` is 196.2, read at runtime by `SkateController`, `PoseController`, `BallService` and `MatchService`;
   - so `skaterTop` + the collider's radius = 28 + 140² / 392.4 + 1 = **78.95**, against glass at 85.
4. **A pass with no target** keeps `BallService`'s lift on (gravity cancelled) until the ball touches something or is caught (`endPass` runs only on touch, catch, loss of the target, out of play).
5. **The goal at gate 2:**
   - the step is half blended there (`Centerline.stepBlend` 0.5);
   - the ring's outline (16 segments, ring 7, rim 1) spans u 36.84–53.16 and y 9.84–26.16;
   - it clears the surface by 7.84 at the closest point;
   - a skater's collider on the upper floor tops out at 5.5.
6. **The arch:** the circle through (0, 8) and (60, 28) has centre (30, 18), radius 31.62 and apex 49.62. At u 45 it is 45.84 high, so it passes over the ring.
7. **Roblox:**
   - "Persistent models and their descendants are never streamed out" (streaming page, 2026-10-23);
   - `Vector3` is native in Luau while `CFrame` and `Vector2` are heap objects (measured in earlier phases);
   - so moving a part through `Position` and `Orientation` allocates nothing.
8. **Code read today:**
   - `TaggedLayout` reads `CornerMarker` as parts and only validates them (no server code reads `markers`);
   - `ArenaParts.parts.markers[g]` is read by `ArenaGlow` (light/restore) and `WorldMarkers` (`gate.Position`);
   - `RideSurfaces.params` serves every client ray: skating, wall leap, poses, camera, ball ground marker;
   - `SkateController.collide` uses it for its cast and its face ray;
   - FrameController renders in this order: input overlays, camera, touch, HUD store, HUD, world markers, arena glow, name tags.

## Review Focus

1. **A ball over the glass top** (a high throw, or an untargeted pass aimed straight up) is out and respawns at once. It never flies on for 15 s.
   - Task 1, Bounds: `puts the ball or a skater out past either glass, under the floor or over the glass top`.
   - Task 1, BallService: the respawn test's over-the-top case.
2. **A glass top lowered, or `hardCap` raised, in a mode's overrides:** the server refuses to build the arena, naming both heights, and builds nothing.
   - Task 1, Geometry: `refuses glass a skater could clear, naming both heights`.
3. **A skater pressing into glass at speed** slides along it and never passes through. A ground ray beside the glass still finds the floor, not the glass.
   - Task 2, SkateController: `slides along glass its collider reaches, which no ground ray sees`.
4. **An art model's corner marker that is a single part, not a Model,** still works for the server's check, the glow and the arrow.
   - Task 3, TaggedLayout: `reads a corner marker that is a model or a part`.
   - Task 3, ArenaParts: the same two shapes.
5. **The carrier skating around a curved end:** the beam stays upright on the outer glass, facing across the track, and hides the moment the ball is thrown.
   - Task 5, CarrierBeam: `moves with the ball around a curved end, upright and facing across the track` and `hides the beam while nobody carries the ball`.

## Decisions not spelled out in the spec (for owner review)

1. **`Geometry.build(config, gravity)` takes gravity as a number** (studs/s², positive). `ArenaService` passes `workspace.Gravity`, or `deps.Gravity` in tests; `Fixtures.GRAVITY` already exists for the rules' tests. Cost if wrong: one parameter.
2. **`Surface.build` returns `{ Piece }`** (no record) now that the roof list is gone. Cost if wrong: none.
3. **`Types.Goal` gains `arm: { Piece }`.** The Builder puts the arm in the `Goal` folder as two ghost parts (no collision, query or touch, visible). Cost if wrong: none.
4. **`TaggedLayout.markers` becomes `{ PVInstance }`.** A marker may be a Model or a part, as an art model may be either. Gates and spawns stay parts. Cost if wrong: none.
5. **`ArenaParts.Parts.markers` becomes `{ [number]: Marker }`**, with `Marker = { parts: { BasePart }, position: Vector3 }`:
   - the arch's own parts, or the part itself;
   - the pivot's position (for an arch, its apex).

   Cost if wrong: one record.
6. **`Fixtures.hudArena` builds each marker as a Model of two parts** with its pivot set, the arch's shape. One test adds a single-part marker. Cost if wrong: none.
7. **Tests that hard-code today's arena numbers move to config-relative helpers.** `Fixtures.outsideArena(config)` gives a point past the outer glass, at a straight's middle. Each spec otherwise derives its numbers from `Config.defaults()` where it can. Cost if wrong: none.
8. **`CarrierBeam` is its own module,** not part of ArenaGlow: ArenaGlow acts only when the HUD's state changes, while the beam moves every frame. Cost if wrong: one module.
9. **The beam hides by `Transparency` 1,** never by reparenting (a write on change). Cost if wrong: none.

**Not in this part:** everything in the spec's §1.3 (Feel, Ball & rules, Presentation, art).
