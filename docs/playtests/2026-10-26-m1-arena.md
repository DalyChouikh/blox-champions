## Publishing notes

- **Republishing:** build `build/game.rbxl` with `rojo build default.project.json --output build/game.rbxl`, open it in Studio, then File > Publish to Roblox As > update the existing "Blox Champions". Each publish was checked by a read-only probe in that window (its place version and a changed module's source).
- **Closing that window:** after version 7 it hung on "Applying script changes", the known Studio bug of the Phase 9 record. After versions 9 and 11 it ignored a close request. Each time its process was ended once the publish had been checked (the owner asked for it, or was told beforehand), and nothing was lost.
