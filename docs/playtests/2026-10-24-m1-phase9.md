## Team checks (Studio's local test, 3 players)

Player2 and Player3 on Orange, Player1 on Blue, placed by server probes and driven through scripted input bindings.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| A pass caught at about 40 studs, on a straight | Pass: it left at 90 studs/s and the teammate caught it 0.35 s later |
| A pass around a bend (the receiver round the curved end) | Caught, but slowly: the ball skimmed the inner wall, slowed under 20 studs/s rolling, and arrived 3.8 s after the throw |
| Attach, on flat floor | Pass: pulled to exactly 4.0 studs behind the leader (`attachFollowDistance`) and held while the leader accelerated to 36 studs/s; released at 3.0 s (`attachMaxSeconds`) going from 36 to 78 studs/s (`attachBoostSpeed` 40) along the leader's facing |
| The ✋ call-pass hand | Pass: on the teammate's screen 0.32 s after the call, gone at 3.32 s (`callPassWindow` 3); never on the opponent's |
| The consoles | Pass: the server's and the three clients' held only their ready lines |

- **The pass around the bend is a fact for the overhaul's Ball & rules part** (Task 4, the tuning pass, was replaced by the overhaul: spec decision A6). A pass leaves straight at the receiver (`Throw.pass`), and its homing adds acceleration only toward the receiver (`Homing.step`). Around the curved end that straight line ran 36 studs from the end's centre, inside the inner wall (38 studs), so the ball met the wall and rolled along it. No homing value steers a pass along the track. The owner's rule for that part: a pass flies straight through the air, and one to a teammate behind a wall hits the wall.
- While attached and moving, the server saw the gap at 6–12 studs: each client's own position reaches the server late.
